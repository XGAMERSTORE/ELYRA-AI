# Realistic character asset requirements

Need a licensed adult humanoid GLB model with skeleton, animations (idle, walk, dance, hug, kiss), blendshapes for mouth/expressions, optimized textures, and mobile LODs. A procedurally assembled mannequin is NOT photorealistic.

Do not redistribute third-party character models without license. Godot can import GLB directly; VRM may require a compatible importer. Prefer a verified GLB with rig and animation clips.
